# Phase 1: Dataset Understanding

## 1. Dataset Overview
- Total rows: 10,194
- Total columns: 21
- Missing values: 0
- Duplicate rows: 0
- Date range: January 3, 2023 – December 30, 2026

## 2. Initial Findings
- Office Supplies has the most records.
- The West region has the most records.
- Consumer is the largest customer segment by record count.
- Standard Class is the most common shipping method.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies have negative total profits.
- Tables has the largest total loss.

## 3. Business Questions
1. Why are Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies generating losses?
2. Which categories and products generate the most sales and profit?
3. What products do customers buy most often?
4. Which regions perform best?
5. How are discounts associated with profit and loss?
6. Which shipping methods are most commonly used?

## 4. Dataset Limitations
- The dataset contains a Discount column but no coupon column.
- Initial findings require further analysis before drawing conclusions.

## 5. Important Columns
- Sales: The money generated by selling a product.
- Profit: The money remaining after accounting for the product's costs.
- Quantity: The number of units sold.
- Category: The main group under which products are classified, such as Furniture, Technology, and Office Supplies.
- Region: The geographical area where a sale took place, such as East, West, Central, or South.
